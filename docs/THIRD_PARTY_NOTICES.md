# Third-party notices and distribution decision

GeoComb bundles or depends on third-party software, including Ultralytics,
PyTorch, OpenCV, SciPy, pandas, openpyxl, Pillow, PySide6/Qt, and PyInstaller.

## Ultralytics

Ultralytics publishes AGPL-3.0 and Enterprise licensing options. Before
distributing GeoComb outside the development team, choose one of the
following routes:

1. distribute GeoComb under terms compatible with AGPL-3.0 and provide the
   corresponding source as required; or
2. obtain an appropriate Ultralytics Enterprise license.

Do not distribute a closed-source executable without resolving this decision.

## PySide6 / Qt for Python

The community edition is offered under LGPLv3/GPLv3, with commercial options
also available. Preserve required notices and review the applicable LGPL/GPL
conditions for the chosen distribution method.

## Model and dataset rights

Confirm that the trained weights and any bundled demonstration images can be
redistributed under the permissions applicable to the underlying training
data and institutional project.

This file is a development checklist, not legal advice.
